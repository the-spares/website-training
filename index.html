Legend of WuKong

Author: Jin He Zai

Translator: Rui Liu

Status: unfinished

Synapsis: Xi You is a very tragic story, it’s a story about a group of people on the road wishing to find their lost desires. They might be weak and small, but they don’t give up hope. The Qi Tian Da Sheng five hundred years ago, now wears a Jin Gu after five hundred years, taking the journey to the West. Forgetting the past, he swings his staff towards his brothers who had fought aside him; what once was the handsome Tian Peng Yuan Shuai, turned into a demon pig. Lover far away in the sky, even if they one day meet, they wouldn’t dare recognize; Jin ChanZi and RuLai gamble on morality, using his own body as the bid, betting on the determination of a resistance heart; Little White Dragon vowed to never marry into the heavens, willingly transforming into a white horse, using a lifetime of silence to repent, accompanying her lover left and right… but there will always be a day, when the heavens will be set on fire, the gods’ power will be threatened again. Because there’s this group of people, they would rather die, then lose.
